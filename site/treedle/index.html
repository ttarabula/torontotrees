Guess the species of today's Toronto street tree. Six tries. New tree every day.

How it works
  • The Street View panorama below shows one real catalogued city-owned tree somewhere in Toronto.
  • Pick a species from the autocomplete and submit. Six guesses total.
  • After each wrong guess: you'll see whether you got the genus right (yellow) or wrong (red), plus one new hint — neighbourhood, then DBH, then bloom timing, then the genus's first letter.
  • Streak resets if you miss a day or fail to guess.
  • Same tree for everyone, same day. Compare scores with friends.
